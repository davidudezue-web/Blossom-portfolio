About Me

Chukwuemeka David Udezue

Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Business Performance Reporting

I believe data becomes valuable when it helps people understand a problem, evaluate their performance, and decide what to do next.

My academic background is in Mathematics and Statistics. I have developed an interest in translating business questions into analytical tasks, organizing data, examining performance indicators, and communicating findings through reports and visualizations.

My professional interests include data analytics, business intelligence, reporting, data visualization, and business analysis. I am particularly interested in opportunities where analytical work supports operational improvement, commercial performance, and evidence-based decision-making.

Alongside my analytics work, I founded Blossom Graphviz Concept and BlossomViz Academy. These initiatives provide opportunities to apply business thinking, creative communication, digital skills, and training.

My Professional Focus

I approach analytics as a process of understanding the business question, preparing the data, examining the evidence, and communicating findings clearly. The objective is to make information useful to the people who need to act on it.

Data Preparation

Business Intelligence

Data Visualization
