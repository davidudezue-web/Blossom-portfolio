Excel & Data Preparation

Preparing business transaction data for reliable analysis requires more than opening a spreadsheet. This project documents the data preparation workflow supporting a sales performance analysis, from initial inspection to validation and reporting readiness.

01. Project Objective

Establish a structured and verifiable preparation process for transaction data before using it to calculate sales KPIs, compare performance, and build business intelligence reports.

The focus is data reliability: understanding the source structure, checking field consistency, validating records, and preparing suitable fields for downstream analysis.

02. Data Preparation Workflow

STAGE 01

Source Inspection

Inspect workbook sheets, column headers, field types, record structure, and the suitability of the source data for analysis.

STAGE 02

Data Quality Checks

Check for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent categories, invalid entries, and unexpected data types.

STAGE 03

Cleaning & Transformation

Apply appropriate cleaning and transformation steps, standardize fields where necessary, and preserve the meaning of source data.

STAGE 04

Validation

Recheck record counts, numeric fields, dates, and summary totals to identify discrepancies before analysis.

STAGE 05

Analysis Readiness

Organize the prepared data for consistent aggregation, KPI calculations, comparisons, and reporting.

STAGE 06

Business Intelligence

Use validated data as the foundation for sales performance measures, visual reporting, and business interpretation.

03. Tools & Techniques

  • Microsoft Excel: spreadsheet inspection, structured data review, and validation.
  • Power Query: repeatable data-cleaning and transformation workflows where applicable.
  • Data validation: checking the consistency and plausibility of fields and summary figures.
  • Power BI: downstream KPI calculations, visual analysis, and performance reporting.

04. Quality Assurance

A reliable analytical workflow should preserve source data, make transformations traceable, and reconcile calculated totals against the relevant records. Any unexplained discrepancies should be investigated before findings are presented to decision-makers.

Data integrity: The original transaction workbook is not published on this page. This public case study describes the analytical workflow without exposing potentially confidential company records.

05. Connection to Business Insights

Data preparation supports the quality of the final analysis. Once the data has been checked and prepared, the next step is to evaluate revenue, transaction activity, product performance, and other relevant indicators in context.

The related sales performance case study demonstrates how prepared data can support business intelligence reporting and communicate findings to stakeholders.
