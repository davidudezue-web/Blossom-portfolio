Project Objective

The objective was to organise sales records into an executive-friendly Power BI report that supports monitoring of recorded sales value, quantity sold, product contribution, customer mix, and branch performance. Interactive filters allow users to explore the data by customer type, transaction, date, and branch.

Business Questions

  • Which products contribute most to recorded sales value?
  • How is recorded sales value distributed across branches?
  • Which customer segments account for the largest share of recorded sales?
  • Which performance indicators should managers monitor?
Scope and limitations
  • 30 recorded transactions covering 3–17 January 2025.
  • Descriptive analysis limited to the supplied records.
  • Sales value is not profit; cost and margin data were not provided.
  • The observation period is short and should not be treated as a long-term trend.

Key Findings and Business Insights

The analysis covers 30 recorded transactions from 3–17 January 2025. The category figures below have been verified against the supplied transaction records and are calculated against total recorded sales value of ₦3,339,100.

Finding 01

Branch Performance

Masaka recorded sales value of ₦1,980,700, approximately 59.32% of total recorded sales value. New Nyanya accounted for ₦1,358,400, approximately 40.68%.

Business implication: Compare transaction counts, units sold, product mix, and average transaction value between branches to investigate the difference in recorded sales value.

Finding 02

Product Contribution

Dangote Cement generated recorded sales value of ₦887,500, approximately 26.58% of the total.

Business implication: Review sales volume, inventory availability, pricing, and purchasing patterns when making product-management decisions.

Finding 03

Customer Segment Contribution

Contractors generated recorded sales value of ₦1,352,400, approximately 40.50% of the total.

Business implication: Examine order frequency, order size, and product preferences to identify opportunities to strengthen customer relationships.

Finding 04

Average Transaction Value

Total recorded sales value of ₦3,339,100 across 30 transactions gives an average transaction value of approximately ₦111,303.33.

Business implication: Monitor average transaction value alongside transaction count and units sold to assess changes in recorded sales performance.

Finding 05

Data Validation

The 30 transaction totals passed the arithmetic check against quantity multiplied by unit price.

Business implication: Maintain validation checks and reconcile dashboard totals with source records. Arithmetic consistency alone does not prove completeness or overall data accuracy.

Interpretation note: The analysis covers a short observation period. It does not establish long-term trends, profitability, causation, or future performance. Recorded sales value should not be interpreted as profit.

Management Recommendations

These are proposed follow-up actions based on the analysis, not claims that the actions have already been implemented.

Recommendation 01

Review Branch Performance

Compare transaction counts, units sold, product mix, and average transaction value across Masaka and New Nyanya.

Purpose: Investigate the factors associated with differences in recorded sales value.

Recommendation 02

Monitor Product Contribution

Review sales volume, inventory availability, pricing, and purchasing patterns for Dangote Cement and other products.

Purpose: Support evidence-based inventory and product-management decisions.

Recommendation 03

Understand Customer Segments

Compare transaction frequency, order size, product preferences, and repeat-purchase behaviour across customer types.

Purpose: Identify opportunities to improve customer relationships and target sales activities.

Recommendation 04

Expand the Reporting Period

Add more historical transactions and report consistently by week or month.

Purpose: Enable more reliable trend analysis and comparisons across reporting periods.

Financial reporting limitation: Validated cost data is required to calculate profitability and profit margins. Recorded sales value alone does not establish profitability.
